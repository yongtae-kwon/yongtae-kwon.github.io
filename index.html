Bio

Welcome! I am a third year PhD student in Public Policy at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by Prof. Mary Evans and Prof. Andrew Waxman . I study the political economy of environmental and energy regulation: why efficient policies are difficult to implement, how regulators enforce rules in practice, and how policy design can improve welfare under political and institutional constraints. I use tools from applied microeconomics and empirical industrial organization to answer these questions.

I received my MPP and BA degree from Seoul National University. Here's a link to my [CV].
